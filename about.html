About CineVault

CineVault is a digital products store built by working video creators. We started with a simple frustration: most "creator packs" sold online are generic, outdated, or impossible to actually use in a real edit.

So we build the packs we wish existed — transitions, LUTs, sound design, templates and workflows that we test in real client projects before they ever reach the store.

What we believe

  • Quality over quantity. A small catalog where every product earns its place.
  • Honest pricing. Professional tools at prices independent creators can afford. No fake discounts, no inflated "original" prices.
  • Buy once, own forever. No subscriptions. Every purchase includes lifetime access and free updates.

Who it's for

Reel editors, YouTubers, TikTok creators, freelancers and small agencies — anyone who edits video and wants to look like a studio without a studio budget.
